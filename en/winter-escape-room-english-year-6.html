Winter Escape Room: English Year 6

The class rescues the winter lantern: six stations, two locks, one magic word. Free and ready to print.

What is inside?

  • Six stations on word classes, verb tenses, sentence parts, spelling strategies, a non-fiction text and a story
  • Two locks (a number lock and a magic word) as the solution, hint cards that never give away the answer
  • Answer key, reflection sheet and certificate, about 90 minutes without a computer
