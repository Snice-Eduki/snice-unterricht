Description

Look through the window – all the way to the center of the Earth! Students build a peek-through pop-up card: crust, mantle, outer core and inner core stand up as parallel panels with round windows, so you can look from the surface right down to the inner core. A to-scale depth bar (0 – 2,900 – 5,100 – 6,370 km) shows how deep each layer goes.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (29 pages):

  • Teacher notes, materials & preparation, lesson plan (about 90 minutes)
  • Assembly instructions in 7 steps with calculated 3D sketches, common mistakes & tips (tab right/wrong)
  • "How it works" page with dimensions and side views at different opening angles
  • Finished example + opening sequence (30°, 60°, 90°, 120°)
  • Base card and panels in color AND black-and-white
  • Info cards and key word list
  • Worksheets in 3 levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging)
  • Fill-in-the-blanks text, quiz (10 questions), assessment sheet
  • Blank templates for students' own pop-ups
  • Complete answer keys

LEARNING GOALS: name and order the four layers of the Earth, describe each layer (solid or liquid, temperature, depth), compare the Earth with a hard-boiled egg, calculate layer thicknesses (level 3).

STANDARDS: background for 4-ESS2-2 (patterns of Earth's features, volcanoes and earthquakes) and MS-ESS2-1 / MS-ESS2-3 (Earth's materials and plate motion).

MATERIALS: cardstock (60–110 lb), scissors, glue stick or liquid craft glue / double-sided tape, colored pencils for the black-and-white version.

TIME: crafting about 40–60 minutes, complete unit about 90 minutes.

PERFECT FOR: earth science units, volcano and earthquake lessons, science centers, STEM activities, interactive notebooks.

Format: A4 PDF – on US Letter print every page with "Fit to page"; all parts shrink together, so the pop-up still works (explained in the PDF). All graphics are original vector drawings made by the author; texts were written with AI assistance and carefully checked by the author.
