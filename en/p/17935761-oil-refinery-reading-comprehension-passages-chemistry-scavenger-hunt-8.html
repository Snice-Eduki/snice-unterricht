Oil Refinery Reading Comprehension Passages | Chemistry Scavenger Hunt 8-9

Science & Health · Grades 8–9 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A chemistry and physical science reading scavenger hunt for 8th and 9th grade! In “Inside an Oil Refinery,” a tall tower rises on the refinery grounds. Lena and Jonas want to know how so many substances are created from a single crude oil, and the chemist Dr. Carter guides them through the process: from the Crude Oil Tank to the Furnace, Bitumen, Heavy Fuel Oil, Diesel, Kerosene, Gasoline, and Refinery Gas. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful refinery map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Oil refinery map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer leads to a picture gate
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short science questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

CHEMISTRY & SCIENCE CONTENT: crude oil and its processing through fractional distillation, including the Crude Oil Tank, Furnace, Bitumen, Heavy Fuel Oil, Diesel, Kerosene, Gasoline, and Refinery Gas (connects to NGSS MS-PS1-3, MS-PS1-4, and MS-PS1.A Structure and Properties of Matter).

SKILLS: close reading and citing textual evidence (RI.8.1 / RI.9-10.1), domain vocabulary (RI.8.4 / RI.9-10.4), following written directions, collaborative discussion (SL.8.1 / SL.9-10.1), and solving a math word problem.

PERFECT FOR: chemistry classes, physical science classes, matter and natural resources units, reading across the curriculum, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
