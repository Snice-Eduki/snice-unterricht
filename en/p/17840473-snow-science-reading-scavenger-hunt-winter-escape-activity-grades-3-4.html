Description

What is snow made of? Find out with this hands-on winter reading scavenger hunt! Friends Ava and Leo set up a little snow lab on a snowy day – and your students investigate with them: from the science table to the catching board, the magnifier station, the thermometer, the snow tower, the snowball press, and the color test, all the way to the results board. Students read a short station, find the clue to the next place on a colorful lab map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Snow lab map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem with picture gates
  • Listening comprehension page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short science questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up experiments) and full answer key

SCIENCE CONTENT: snowflakes and their six-sided shape, why the cardboard must be cold, when water freezes, why a blanket of snow insulates plants and small animals, wet snow vs. powder snow, and why snow looks white although ice is clear (builds on NGSS 2-PS1-1 and 2-PS1-4, connects to 3-ESS2-1). Science practices: observing, measuring, comparing, recording results.

SKILLS: close reading and finding key details (RI.3.1 / RI.4.1), following written directions, listening comprehension (SL.3.2 / SL.4.2), addition, subtraction, and multiplication facts within 32.

PERFECT FOR: January and winter literacy centers, snow day science, STEM and ELA integration, early finishers, substitute plans, and small groups. Works in warm climates too – no real snow needed.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
