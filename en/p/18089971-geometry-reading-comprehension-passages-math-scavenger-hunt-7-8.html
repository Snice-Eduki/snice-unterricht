Math · Grades 7–8 · Reading trail

A math and reading scavenger hunt for 7th and 8th grade! In “The Gingerbread Geometry Lab,” grade 7 students prepare gingerbread houses in the School Kitchen for the Holiday Market – and your students join them: from the School Kitchen to the Template Table, Baking Sheet, Cutting Board, Icing Station, Cooling Rack, and Decorating Table, all the way to the Holiday Market. Emir wants to start cutting right away, while Lotta insists on careful measuring. Their math teacher, Mr. Brandl, shows the plan, and Crumbs, a little talking gingerbread man, crumbles when measurements are inaccurate. Students read passages, follow clues to the next place, complete tasks, and navigate the scavenger hunt. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (23 pages):

  • Reading scavenger hunt centered on rectangular prisms, nets, surface area, and volume
  • 8 stations: School Kitchen, Template Table, Baking Sheet, Cutting Board, Icing Station, Cooling Rack, Decorating Table, and Holiday Market
  • 4 dead ends: Sink, Trash Can, Windowsill, and Broom Closet
  • 3 task types: matching, quiz, and riddle
  • Math word problem whose answer leads students through a picture gate

MATH CONTENT: rectangular prisms, nets, surface area, and volume in a winter gingerbread-house context (connects to CCSS.Math.Content.7.G.B.6).

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.7.1 / RI.8.1), domain vocabulary (RI.7.4 / RI.8.4), following written directions, collaborative discussion (SL.7.1 / SL.8.1), and applying geometry concepts.

PERFECT FOR: math classes, geometry units, reading across the curriculum, winter lessons, and seasonal classroom activities.

Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
