Christmas Carols Reading Comprehension Passage | Christmas Activity Grades 5-8

Music · Grades 5–8 · Reading comprehension worksheet

$2.50View on TpT

Bring Christmas carols into your classroom with this no-prep nonfiction reading comprehension resource! Students read a short informational passage and then show what they understood in three engaging activities – perfect for the weeks before winter break.

From the text: "Christmas carols are special songs that people sing during the festive season, especially around Christmas time."

What's included (5 pages, answer keys included):

  • Page 1: Informational passage about Christmas carols as a cloze activity with a word bank (8 gaps)
  • Page 2: Answer key for the cloze activity
  • Page 3: Listening comprehension – students scan the QR code to hear an audio recording of the text (free MP3, no login needed), then write a title, note three facts, and summarize the recording in two sentences
  • Page 4: Word search with the 8 key vocabulary words
  • Page 5: Answer key for the word search

Why teachers love it:

  • Clear, simple English (about CEFR A2–B1) – ideal for grades 5–8, ESL/ELL students, and reading intervention
  • Great for December literacy centers, early finishers, sub plans, and homework
  • Black-and-white and photocopy-friendly – just print and go
