Math Mosaic FREE Sampler | Multiplication Color Puzzle | Grades 2-3

Math · Grades 2–3 · Resource

FREEView on TpT

Math practice with a surprise! In this FREE Math Mosaic, students solve 16 multiplication problems, cut out 16 picture tiles and glue each tile onto the problem with the matching answer. When all the lines fit together, a hedgehog picture appears - students can check their own work and then color the picture.

Three levels, same picture:

  • Level 1: 2s and 10s
  • Level 2: 3s, 4s and 5s
  • Level 3: 6s and 7s

What is included (12 pages):

  • Instructions with math tips
  • 3 problem grids (16 problems each) with glue boxes
  • 3 tile sheets with 16 answer tiles each (all answers on a sheet are different)
  • Answer picture with coloring page
  • Error detective, I-can check and an invent-your-own mosaic page
  • Answer keys for all three levels and a teacher page

Great for math centers, early finishers, sub plans, the days before holidays, intervention groups and homework. About 45 minutes. Print single-sided; you need scissors and glue sticks.
