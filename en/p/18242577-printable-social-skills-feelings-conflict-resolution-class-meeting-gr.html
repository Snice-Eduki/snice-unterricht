Printable Social Skills: Feelings, Conflict Resolution, Class Meeting, Gr. 1-4

General Knowledge · Grades 1–4 · Resource

$3.99View on TpT

Learning to get along: with this booklet of posters and cards, students recognize their feelings, practice I-messages, solve conflicts in five steps and run their own class meeting.

Included (15 pages + cover):

  • Overview and notes for the teacher (1 page)
  • Poster: our eight feelings to hang in the classroom
  • Poster: solving conflicts in 5 steps
  • Recognizing feelings, writing or drawing when I feel this way
  • I-messages: circle, complete and write your own
  • Conflict stories: choose the best solution and explain why
  • Kind or unkind? Sorting sentences
  • Class rules, compliment cards and class meeting minutes template
  • Feelings tracker, 12 game cards, self-assessment and certificate
  • Answer key and notes (1 page)

Use in class: Grades 1 to 4, in social-emotional learning, morning meetings and class meetings, at learning stations or as emergency sub plans. All pages are reproducible, can be used on their own and have a space for name, class and date. A drawn guide character accompanies students on every page.

Texts, illustrations and layout are my own work, AI-assisted and checked by me. No photos, no real people, no brands.

Have fun learning together! Snice :)
