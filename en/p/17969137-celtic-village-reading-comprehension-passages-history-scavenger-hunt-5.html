Celtic Village Reading Comprehension Passages | History Scavenger Hunt 5-6

History & Society · Grades 5–6 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

An ancient history and reading scavenger hunt for 5th and 6th grade! In “Life in a Celtic Village,” Mira, Toby, and Can visit an open-air museum with a reconstructed Celtic village – and your students join them: from the Museum Gate to the Longhouse, Weaving Hut, Forge, Trading Square, Pottery Shop, Herb Garden, Burial Mound, Samhain Fire, and Fortification. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful Celtic village map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Celtic village map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer leads to a picture gate
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short history questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

HISTORY CONTENT: daily life in an Iron Age Celtic village, including reconstructed buildings, ancient techniques, crafts, trade, burial practices, Samhain, and fortifications. Experimental archaeologist Mr. Brandl guides Mira, Toby, and Can through the site, beginning at the Museum Gate, where Can receives a small carved Wooden Horse. Connects to CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RI.5.1, RI.5.3, RI.5.4, RI.5.7; CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.SL.5.1; and C3 Framework D2.His.9.3-5, D2.His.10.3-5, D2.His.12.3-5.

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.5.1), relationships between historical events, ideas, and concepts (RI.5.3), domain vocabulary (RI.5.4), integrating information from text and visuals (RI.5.7), collaborative discussion (SL.5.1), following written directions, and historical thinking.

PERFECT FOR: ancient history, world history, social studies, Iron Age units, fall lessons, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
