Window Art Workshop Shapes Counting Role Play | Winter Math Center Grade 1

Math · Grades 1–2 · Math role play

$5.00View on TpT

Turn your classroom into The Window Art Workshop! In this math role-play for Grade 1, students create stars, candles, and snowmen using circles, triangles, squares, and rectangles. They count shapes, continue patterns, and complete mirrored images. This pack is fully paper-based (no devices).

I have designed this with three levels so all children can play together at one table while working at their own level:

★ full image, numbers provided

★★ count independently, double, add

★★★ complete half image (mirroring)

Included (26 pages + cover):

  • Teacher notes with lesson flow, curriculum alignment, copy plan, and a completed example
  • 3 Blueprint Cards (★/★★/★★★) with 8 illustrated items
  • Stand sign, role cards (Shapes · Counting · Mirroring), and sentence strips for ELL students
  • 3 Build Slips with "I can" checklist and partner check
  • 12 Task Cards
  • Challenge Window with 9 tasks for fast finishers
  • Coloring Page: Window Art for fast finishers
  • Color-by-number in three levels (★ plus and minus to 10 · ★★ doubling to 20 · ★★★ completing to 20) – the result reveals the color, and a candle in the window appears at the end
  • Answer keys for self-checking (including colored images for color-by-number)
  • "Shape Pro" certificate in color and B&W

All illustrations are my own, created with AI support and checked by me.
