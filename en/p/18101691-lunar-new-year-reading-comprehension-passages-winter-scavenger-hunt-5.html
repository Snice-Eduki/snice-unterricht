Lunar New Year Reading Comprehension Passages | Winter Scavenger Hunt 5-6

Holidays & Traditions · Grades 5–6 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A reading and social studies scavenger hunt for 5th and 6th grade! In “Lunar New Year at Lin's Home,” a student is invited to celebrate Lunar New Year with friend Lin for the first time – and your students join the visit: from the Front Door to the Broom Closet, Living Room, Calendar Wall, Kitchen, Dining Table, Balcony, and Courtyard. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful home map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 24 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Home map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem with picture gates
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short reading questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

LUNAR NEW YEAR CONTENT: a first-time visit to Lin’s home for a Lunar New Year celebration, explored through reading stations around the home and courtyard (connects to C3 Framework D2.Civ.10.3-5 and D2.Civ.10.6-8).

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.5.1 / RI.6.1), relationships between ideas and events (RI.5.3 / RI.6.3), vocabulary in context (RI.5.4 / RI.6.4), following written directions, collaborative discussion (SL.5.1 / SL.6.1), matching, quiz, riddle solving, and a math word problem.

PERFECT FOR: reading classes, social studies lessons, Lunar New Year units, winter activities, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
