Description

Get your students reading, thinking, and moving with this winter reading scavenger hunt! Leo and his mom follow animal tracks through a snowy forest – and your students follow them, too. Students read a short station, find the clue to the next place on a colorful forest map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Forest map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem with picture gates
  • Listening comprehension page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short science questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step) and full answer key

SCIENCE CONTENT: how fresh snow shows tracks; the track patterns of the fox, deer, hare, small birds, mouse, and squirrel; what tracks tell us about animals in winter (connects to NGSS 4-LS1-1 animal structures).

SKILLS: close reading and finding key details (RI.3.1 / RI.4.1), following written directions, listening comprehension (SL.3.2 / SL.4.2), addition, subtraction, and multiplication within 50.

PERFECT FOR: literacy centers, science and ELA integration, winter activities, early finishers, substitute plans, and small groups.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
