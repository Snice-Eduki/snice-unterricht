Free Time Reading Comprehension Passages | Life Skills Scavenger Hunt 5-6

Music · Grades 5–6 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A reading and life skills scavenger hunt for 5th and 6th grade! In “My Free Time Balance,” Lotte and Ben have a free Saturday and visit an open house at the lakeside community center – and your students join them: from the Information Booth to the Music School, Climbing Wall, Craft Workshop, Reading Corner, Lake Shore, Youth Fire Dept, Hammock Grove, and Campfire Circle. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful community center map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (23 pages):

  • Reading scavenger hunt with stations including the Information Booth, Music School, Climbing Wall, Craft Workshop, Reading Corner, Lake Shore, Youth Fire Dept, Hammock Grove, and Campfire Circle
  • Lakeside community center setting with Flip, Lotte and Ben’s little paper airplane, flying ahead
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer leads to a picture gate
  • Dead ends at the Ticket Booth, Parking Lot, Trash Bins, and Boat Rental

LIFE SKILLS CONTENT: exploring different ways to spend free time through music, climbing, crafts, reading, time by the lake, community involvement, relaxation, and shared activities.

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.5.1 / RI.6.1), following written directions, discussion and collaboration (SL.5.1 / SL.6.1), problem solving, and a math word problem.

PERFECT FOR: reading classes, life skills lessons, summer learning, early finishers, and substitute plans.

No prep beyond printing. Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
