Description

Roll – draw – discover! Five dice games let students create their own artwork in the style of five famous art movements and artists. Every roll decides a shape, a line or a color – so every picture is unique, and students learn what makes each style special.

THE 5 STYLES:

1. Cubism – a cubist face (front and side view at once)

2. Wassily Kandinsky – circles and color sounds

3. Piet Mondrian – grid pictures with red, blue and yellow

4. Paul Klee – a town of colored squares

5. Franz Marc – an animal in expressive colors

WHAT'S INCLUDED (53 pages):

  • For each style: dice chart with rules, sample round "how a picture grows", drawing template with roll log, style info card, artwork talk, reflection cards (with expert question for older students), assessment sheet
  • Craft die with art symbols + classic dot die template (correct dice: opposite sides add up to 7) – for classes without enough dice
  • Teacher notes, materials list, lesson schedule (about 90 minutes per style), common mistakes & tips
  • Finished examples of all 5 styles
  • Experiment page and style comparison, gallery labels for a class exhibition
  • Detailed answer keys

LEARNING GOALS: recognize characteristics of art styles, use shape, line and color on purpose, talk about art using art vocabulary, reflect on one's own artwork.

MATERIALS: one die per student or table (or the craft die), pencil, black marker, ruler, colored pencils, crayons or watercolors.

PERFECT FOR: art lessons, art history units, sub plans, early finishers, art centers, end-of-year activities.

Format: A4 PDF – prints on US Letter as well. Only public-domain artists. All graphics are original vector drawings made by the author; texts were written with AI assistance and carefully checked by the author.
