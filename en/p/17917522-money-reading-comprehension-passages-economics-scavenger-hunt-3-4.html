Money Reading Comprehension Passages | Economics Scavenger Hunt 3-4

History & Society · Grades 3–4 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A reading and economics scavenger hunt for 3rd and 4th grade! In “Smart Money at the Fall Market,” Ida and Malik from 3rd grade go to the fall market in Moosbach with Grandpa Kurt – and your students join them: from the Market Fountain to the Toy Stand, Bakery Stand, Card Stand, School Supply Stand, Bank, Bookmobile, and Café. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful fall market map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (23 pages):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Fall market map with the story locations and dead ends
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer leads to a picture gate
  • Teacher guide and answer key

ECONOMICS & MONEY CONTENT: making thoughtful choices about spending money, comparing benefits and costs of individual choices, and recognizing incentives that can influence decisions (connects to C3 Framework D2.Eco.1.3-5 and D2.Eco.2.3-5).

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.3.1 / RI.4.1), understanding relationships among events, ideas, and procedures (RI.3.3 / RI.4.3), collaborative discussion (SL.3.1 / SL.4.1), and solving a money word problem (3.OA.D.8 / 4.OA.A.3 where corresponding multistep money problems are used).

PERFECT FOR: reading comprehension, economics and money units, fall lessons, early finishers, and substitute plans.

No prep beyond printing. Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
