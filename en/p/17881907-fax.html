Fax – reading comprehension worksheet with cloze text, listening task and word search (5 pages, answer keys included).

From the text: "Long ago, people needed a way to send exact copies of papers, like letters or pictures, to someone far away without mailing them."

What's included:

  • Page 1: Informational text about Fax in English as a cloze text with a word bank (8 gaps)
  • Page 2: Answer key for the cloze text
  • Page 3: Listening comprehension – scan the QR code to play the English audio recording of the text (free MP3, no login), then write a title, note three facts and summarise the recording in two sentences
  • Page 4: Word search with the 8 key words
  • Page 5: Answer key for the word search

The text is written in clear, simple English (approx. CEFR A2–B1), which makes it ideal for grades 5–8, ESL/EFL learners, reading intervention, substitute plans, homework or early finishers. No prep: print and go. Black-and-white, photocopy-friendly.
