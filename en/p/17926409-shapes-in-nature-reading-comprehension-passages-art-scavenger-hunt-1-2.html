Shapes in Nature Reading Comprehension Passages | Art Scavenger Hunt 1-2

Art & Culture · Grades 1–2 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A reading and visual arts scavenger hunt for 1st and 2nd grade! In “Shapes in Nature,” Pina and Caleb’s second-grade class goes on an art trip to a nature park – and your students join them: from the Forest Edge to Snail Rock, Flower Meadow, Bee House, Creek Bank, Tree Stump, and Art Spot. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful nature park map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Nature park map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: quiz, matching, and a riddle + a math word problem with picture gates
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording and answer questions
  • Puzzle page
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

READING & ART CONTENT: a spring art trip through a nature park where Pina and Caleb explore shapes in nature with their little stuffed snail Shelly, moving through the Forest Edge, Snail Rock, Flower Meadow, Bee House, Creek Bank, Tree Stump, and Art Spot.

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.1.1 / RI.2.1), vocabulary in context (RI.1.4 / RI.2.4), following written directions, listening and collaborative discussion (SL.1.1, SL.1.2 / SL.2.1, SL.2.2), visual arts connections (VA:Cr1.1.1a, VA:Cr2.1.1a, VA:Cr1.1.2a, VA:Cr2.1.2a), and solving a math word problem.

PERFECT FOR: reading comprehension practice, visual arts classes, spring activities, early finishers, and substitute plans.

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
