Description

A screen-free coding reading scavenger hunt for 1st and 2nd grade! In “Coding with a Floor Robot,” Ava and Theo’s second-grade class has Robot Day with their teacher, Mr. Walker – and your students join in: from the story circle to the button board, the grid rug, the arrow card table, the tape grid, the obstacle course, and the bug table, all the way to the treasure island. Along the way, they learn what a command is, why a program is a list of commands in order, and how to find and fix a bug. Students read a short station, find the clue to the next place on a colorful classroom map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Robot room map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem (adding and subtracting within 10) with picture gates
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short coding questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, unplugged follow-up ideas) and full answer key

CODING CONTENT (unplugged): commands and buttons, one square per command, turning in place, a program as a sequence of commands, why order matters, exact instructions (“human robot”), planning a path, and debugging (connects to CSTA 1A-AP-08, 1A-AP-10, 1A-AP-14). The robot is fictional – no brand, no device needed.

SKILLS: reading for key details (RI.1.1 / RI.2.1), reading with purpose and understanding (RF.1.4 / RF.2.4), listening comprehension (SL.1.2 / SL.2.2), positional words, adding and subtracting within 10.

PERFECT FOR: Hour of Code and Computer Science Education Week, STEM centers, technology specials, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two or three lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
