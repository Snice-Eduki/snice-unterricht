Muscles Reading Comprehension Passages | Biology Scavenger Hunt 7-8

Animals & Nature · Grades 7–8 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A biology and life science reading scavenger hunt for 7th and 8th grade! In “Muscles at Work,” Mia and Tom want to find out how our arm can bend and straighten again – and your students join them: from the Upper Tendon to the Flexor, Muscle Belly, Muscle Fiber, Lower Tendon, Extensor, Blood Vessel, and Nerve. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful muscle map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Muscle map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: quiz, matching, and a riddle + a math word problem with a picture gate
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short science questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

BIOLOGY & SCIENCE CONTENT: skeletal muscle structure, including tendons, the muscle belly, and muscle fibers; how the flexor and extensor work as opposing muscles to bend and straighten the arm; and how blood vessels and nerves interact with muscle tissue (connects to NGSS MS-LS1-3).

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RST.6-8.1, RI.7.1 / RI.8.1), following written directions, speaking and listening (SL.7.1 / SL.8.1), and solving a math word problem to identify a picture gate.

PERFECT FOR: biology classes, life science classes, human body units, skeletal muscle lessons, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.

U.S. STANDARDS (alignment):

  • RI.7.1; RI.7.4; RL.7.1; SL.7.2; L.7.4; RI.8.1; RI.8.4; RL.8.1; SL.8.2; L.8.4; RST.6-8.4; RH.6-8.4; NGSS MS-LS1-1; NGSS MS-LS1-3; C3 D2.Geo.1.6-8; C3 D2.Geo.2.6-8; VA:Cr1.1.7a; VA:Cr2.1.7a
  • Frameworks: Common Core State Standards for ELA/Literacy (CCSS); Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS); C3 Framework for Social Studies State Standards (NCSS); National Core Arts Standards (Visual Arts)
