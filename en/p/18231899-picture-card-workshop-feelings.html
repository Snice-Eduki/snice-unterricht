Picture Card Workshop: Feelings – Memory Game (FREE)

General Knowledge · Grades 1–3 · Reading comprehension worksheet

FREEView on TpT

Memory game on the topic Feelings: 12 picture cards and 12 word cards make 12 pairs. With rules, sentence patterns for speaking, a game idea and a card back for double-sided printing. With feeling groups shown by color and symbol (readable in b/w, too).

Includes a teacher page with exact preparation (quantity per group, pages, color, laminating, cutting, time).

For Grades 1–3. A matching extension with clip cards and practice pages is available.

Texts AI-assisted and checked by me; the illustrations are my own AI-assisted graphics (no photos).

Happy teaching! Snice :)

Classroom use: ideal for stations, vocabulary support in the primary grades, English language learners and small-group work. The preparation is described exactly (quantity, pages, color or black and white, laminating, cutting, time needed), so you can start within a few minutes. All illustrations are original graphics, with no photos and no well-known characters.

Included (9 pages): Teacher info · Game rules · Pictures only · Words only · Card backs · Answer key
