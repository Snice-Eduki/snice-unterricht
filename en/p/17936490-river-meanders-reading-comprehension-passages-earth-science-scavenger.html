River Meanders Reading Comprehension Passages | Earth Science Scavenger Hunt 7-8

Science & Health · Grades 7–8 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

An earth science and geography reading scavenger hunt for 7th and 8th grade! In “River Power and Meanders,” Mia and Tom explore why a river rarely flows bone-straight – and your students join them: from the Cut Bank to the Point Bar, Sandbar, Oxbow Lake, Riparian Forest, Bank Protection, and River Island, all the way to the Tributary. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful river map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • River map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: quiz, matching, and a riddle + a math word problem with picture gates
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer questions
  • Puzzle page
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide and full answer key

EARTH SCIENCE & GEOGRAPHY CONTENT: river meanders and river features explored through 8 stations: Cut Bank, Point Bar, Sandbar, Oxbow Lake, Riparian Forest, Bank Protection, River Island, and Tributary (connects to NGSS MS-ESS2-2).

SKILLS: reading and following multistep procedures (RST.6-8.3), integrating written information with a visual map (RST.6-8.7), following written directions, and solving a math word problem to identify a picture gate.

PERFECT FOR: earth science classes, geography classes, river units, reading practice, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
