Cookie Baking Reading Comprehension Passages | Life Skills Scavenger Hunt 5-6

Holidays & Traditions · Grades 5–6 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A Christmas baking and kitchen hygiene reading scavenger hunt for 5th and 6th grade! In “Baking Christmas Cookies,” Lottie from Room 5b is baking cookies for the holiday bake sale with Emir and their class, guided by Ms. Kowalski in the school kitchen. Rolly, the cheerful rolling pin mascot, comes along too, and your students join the team. They go from the hand sink to the recipe wall, pantry, kitchen scale, mixing bowl, refrigerator, pastry board, hot oven, and icing table, all the way to the cookie tin shelf. Students read short passages, find the clue to the next place on the kitchen map, and complete a task at every station. Dead ends (trash can, dishwasher, window sill, and broom closet) let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt with 10 stations and 4 dead ends
  • Kitchen map that students use to find the next station
  • 3 built-in task types: matching, quiz, and riddle
  • A math word problem whose answer number leads students to a picture gate
  • Teacher guide and answer key

KITCHEN & LIFE SKILLS CONTENT: handwashing and kitchen safety, reading and following a recipe, measuring in grams, using a kitchen scale, the 1-2-3 dough ratio, and why the order of steps in a procedure matters. Students also learn the vocabulary tare, chill, and preheat. The hunt connects to NHES Standard 7 and the National Family and Consumer Sciences Standards (food preparation, safety, and sanitation).

SKILLS: quoting accurately from a text (RI.5.1), explaining relationships between steps in a procedure (RI.5.3), domain-specific vocabulary (RI.5.4), locating information with a map and text (RI.5.7), collaborative discussion (SL.5.1), converting and measuring units (5.MD.A.1), and ratios and proportional reasoning (6.RP.A.3).

PERFECT FOR: reading centers, the weeks before winter break, holiday bake sale projects, life skills and family and consumer science lessons, early finishers, and substitute plans.

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
