Description

Pull the string – and a flat net folds itself into a 3D shape! In this hands-on geometry craft, students thread a string through numbered holes, pull, and watch a cube, prism or pyramid pop up. Numbers show the threading order and letters show where the corners meet, so every shape works on the first try.

WHAT'S INCLUDED (ZIP with color + black-and-white version, 46 pages each, US Letter):

  • Build sheets + explorer cards for 6 solids: cube, rectangular prism, triangular prism, square pyramid, cylinder and cone (cylinder and cone as roll-and-glue shapes)
  • Bonus: pull-string number cube
  • Blank cube and rectangular prism nets to plan the holes yourself + 1 cm grid paper
  • Picture instructions, "what the finished shapes look like", materials, timing, common mistakes & tips
  • Shape fact cards + fill-in fact card
  • Matching game: which net belongs to which solid?
  • Which nets fold into a cube? (11 cube nets + non-nets)
  • Vertices, edges, faces chart
  • Worksheets in 3 levels: recognizing solids, understanding cube nets, net challenges
  • Shapes Around Us photo hunt, assessment (25 points) and self-assessment
  • Complete answer keys and completed samples
  • Teacher notes with lesson flow (2 × 45 minutes)

LEARNING GOALS: identify and describe 3D shapes, count vertices, edges and faces, connect nets with solids, spatial reasoning and fine motor skills.

STANDARDS: supports CCSS 1.G.A.1, 2.G.A.1 (shapes and their attributes, faces) and prepares 6.G.A.4 (nets of 3D figures).

MATERIALS: cardstock (65–110 lb), crochet thread or thin twine, push pin + foam pad, blunt yarn needle, scissors, ruler, glue stick (cylinder and cone only).

PERFECT FOR: geometry units, math centers, STEM/STEAM days, early finishers, hands-on review. All graphics are original vector drawings made by the author; texts were written with AI assistance and carefully checked by the author.
