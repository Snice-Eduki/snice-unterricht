Winter Wildlife Reading Comprehension Passages | Science Scavenger Hunt 3-4

Science & Health · Grades 3–4 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A winter wildlife and life science reading scavenger hunt for 3rd and 4th grade! In “White as Winter Snow,” Jan and his sister Lina go wildlife watching in the snow-covered mountains – and your students join them: from the Forest Edge to the Snowfield, Boulder, Snowy Thicket, Tree Stump, Mountain Meadow, and Lookout Rock. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful winter mountain map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (23 pages):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Winter mountain map with the wildlife-watching locations
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer leads to a picture gate
  • Correct route through 7 stations, plus 4 dead ends for self-checking
  • Winter wildlife theme with Jan and Lina exploring the snow-covered mountains

SCIENCE CONTENT: winter wildlife observation and how some animals can be difficult to spot against a snowy environment, connecting reading comprehension with NGSS 3-LS3-2 and 3-LS4-2.

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.3.1 / RI.4.1), relationships between events and ideas (RI.3.3 / RI.4.3), using information from illustrations and text (RI.3.7 / RI.4.7), collaborative discussion (SL.3.1 / SL.4.1), following written directions, matching, quiz questions, riddles, and mathematical problem solving.

PERFECT FOR: reading classes, winter units, life science lessons, wildlife topics, early finishers, and seasonal classroom activities.

No prep beyond printing. Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
