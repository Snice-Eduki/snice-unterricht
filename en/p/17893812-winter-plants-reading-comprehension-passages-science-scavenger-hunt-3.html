Winter Plants Reading Comprehension Passages | Science Scavenger Hunt 3-4

Animals & Nature · Grades 3–4 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A reading and science scavenger hunt for 3rd and 4th grade! In ‘Winter Park Mystery Hunt,’ Grandma Rose and her granddaughter Sofia want to find out what green globes are glowing high up in the trees on a cold winter day when all other trees have lost their leaves – and your students join them: from the Park Entrance to the Apple Tree, the Tree Fork, the Berry Branch, the Bird Tree, and the Lookout Bench. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful park map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends (like the Garden Shed, Dog Park, Fountain, or Trash Can) let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Park map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: quiz, matching, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer number leads to a picture gate
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short science questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

SCIENCE CONTENT: A reading comprehension scavenger hunt about winter plants, focusing on how organisms survive in their environments during winter (connects to NGSS 3-LS4-3).

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.3.1 / RI.4.1), following written directions, listening comprehension, and solving a math word problem.

PERFECT FOR: science and reading classes, winter plant units, indoor recess, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
