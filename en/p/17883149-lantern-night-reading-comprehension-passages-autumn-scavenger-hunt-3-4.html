Lantern Night Reading Comprehension Passages | Autumn Scavenger Hunt 3-4

Holidays & Traditions · Grades 3–4 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A fall reading scavenger hunt for 3rd and 4th grade! In “Lantern Night Adventure,” evening is falling and the lanterns are glowing as Ben and his big brother Jonah join a lantern festival – and your students join them: from the Craft Room to the Light Booth, Lantern Pole, Meeting Point, Streetlight, Lighted Path, Windowsill, Music Cart, and Lantern Overlook, all the way to Festival Square. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful festival map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 23 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • Lantern festival map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: quiz, matching, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer leads to a picture gate
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short fall questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

FALL CONTENT: an autumn evening lantern festival with glowing lanterns and a route through festival locations including the Craft Room, Light Booth, Meeting Point, Streetlight, Lighted Path, Music Cart, Lantern Overlook, and Festival Square.

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.3.1 / RI.4.1), connections between events and ideas (RI.3.3 / RI.4.3), using information from illustrations (RI.3.7 / RI.4.7), following written directions, and collaborative discussion (SL.3.1 / SL.4.1).

PERFECT FOR: reading classes, fall units, seasonal literacy centers, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.

U.S. STANDARDS (alignment):

  • RI.3.1; RI.3.4; RL.3.1; SL.3.2; L.3.4; RI.4.1; RI.4.4; RL.4.1; SL.4.2; L.4.4; NGSS 3-PS2-1; NGSS 3-PS2-3; NGSS 4-PS3-2; NGSS 4-PS4-1; C3 D2.Geo.1.3-5; C3 D2.Geo.2.3-5; VA:Cr1.1.3a; VA:Cr2.1.3a
  • Frameworks: Common Core State Standards for ELA/Literacy (CCSS); Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS); C3 Framework for Social Studies
