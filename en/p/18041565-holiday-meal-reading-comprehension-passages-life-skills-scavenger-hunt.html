Holiday Meal Reading Comprehension Passages | Life Skills Scavenger Hunt 7-8

Science & Health · Grades 7–8 · Reading trail

$5.00View on TpT

A reading and life skills scavenger hunt for 7th and 8th grade! In “Holiday Meal Mission,” Lucas and his class prepare a holiday meal in the school kitchen before winter break – and your students join them: from the Bulletin Board to the Cookbook Shelf, Pantry, Refrigerator, Countertop, Stove, and Sink, all the way to the Dining Table. Students read short reading comprehension passages, find the clue to the next place on a colorful school kitchen map, collect a letter at every correct station, and unscramble the letters to crack the secret word. Dead ends let students check their own work – just like an escape room on paper.

WHAT’S INCLUDED (color + black-and-white, 24 pages each):

  • Reading scavenger hunt in 3 differentiated levels (★ easy, ★★ medium, ★★★ challenging) – same story and map, so you can differentiate in one class
  • School kitchen map (landscape, full page) with letter badges
  • 3 built-in tasks: matching, quiz, and a riddle + a math word problem whose answer leads to a picture gate
  • Listening page – students scan the QR code in the PDF to play the recording (MP3) and answer multiple-choice and open questions
  • Puzzle page: word search + short questions
  • Coloring page
  • Teacher guide (standards, prep, timing, step-by-step, follow-up ideas) and full answer key

LIFE SKILLS & HEALTH CONTENT: planning, shopping, cooking, and staying on schedule while preparing a holiday meal, with connections to health and well-being (National Health Education Standards 1 and 7).

SKILLS: close reading and key details (RI.7.1 / RI.8.1), analyzing interactions and connections among ideas and events (RI.7.3 / RI.8.3), collaborative discussion (SL.7.1 / SL.8.1), following written directions, listening comprehension, and proportional reasoning (7.RP.A.2).

PERFECT FOR: reading classes, life skills lessons, health classes, winter activities, holiday-themed lessons, early finishers, and substitute plans.

TIME: about 90 minutes (can be split over two lessons).

No prep beyond printing (laminating the map is optional). Some illustrations were created with AI tools and reviewed by the author.
