The Travel Agency Map Scale Role Play | Winter Math Center Grade 4

Math · Grades 4–5 · Math role play

$5.00View on TpT

I turn my classroom into the Mountain View Travel Agency! In this math role-play for Grade 4, students read map distances, convert using the scale in kilometers and meters, and plan winter trips—round trips and multi-day itineraries—all without a computer or tablet.

I have designed this with three levels so all children can play together at the same table while working at their own level:

★ 1 cm = 1 km, whole kilometers

★★ 1 : 100,000 with cm and mm, km and m

★★★ 1 : 200,000, routes and multi-day trips

This pack is fully paper-based (no devices). All illustrations are my own, created with AI support and checked by me.

Included (26 pages + cover):

  • Teacher notes with lesson flow, curriculum alignment (LehrplanPLUS Bavaria), copy plan, and a completed example
  • 3 Travel Catalogs (★/★★/★★★) with 8 illustrated items
  • Sign, role cards (Travel Advisor · Map Pro · Booking Clerk), and sentence strips for ELL students
  • 3 Booking Sheets with "I can" checklist and partner check
  • 12 Task Cards
  • Challenge Peak with 9 problems for fast finishers
  • Coloring Page: Winter Mountain Trip for fast finishers
  • Color-by-number in three levels (★ multiplication (×) tasks · ★★ kilometers and meters · ★★★ large multiplication (×) tasks) – the result reveals a suitcase in front of snowy mountains
  • Answer keys for self-checking (including colored images for the color-by-number)
  • Trip Planning Pro certificate in color and B&W
